# Window manager chooser image sources

The chooser cards are composed over the CatOS dark wallpaper and use upstream
project artwork:

- `sway.png`: Sway logo from `swaywm/sway`, MIT.
- `labwc.png`: Labwc logo from `labwc/labwc`, GPL-2.0-only.
- `wayfire.png`: Wayfire logo from `WayfireWM/wf-shell`, MIT.
- `deepin.png`: Deepin logo from `linuxdeepin/deepin-desktop-base`, GPL-3.0-or-later.

The cards are generated assets; their text and composition are CatOS project
work. The original project names and logos remain trademarks of their owners.
